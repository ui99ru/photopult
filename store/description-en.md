# Photopult — store description (EN)

## Short description (~80 chars)

Control one phone's camera from another. No internet, no router required.

## Full description

**Photopult** turns two Android phones into a camera and its remote. Put one phone on a tripod —
it takes the photo; hold the other one — it shows a live preview with zoom, shutter and timer.
Perfect for group shots where you want to be in the frame, for shooting from a distance, and for
shake-free photos without touching the camera.

The link is **direct between the phones** — no Wi-Fi router, no mobile internet, no sign-up and
no accounts. The phones find each other over Bluetooth and connect over a fast Wi-Fi channel
(Google Nearby Connections).

**Features:**

- 📷 Live camera preview on the remote — see the shot before you take it
- 🔍 Zoom and front/back camera switch from the remote
- ⏱ 3s and 10s timer with a large countdown on both screens
- 🔥 Burst mode — hold the shutter button
- ⚡ Flash control (off / auto / on)
- 🎯 Tap-to-focus on the preview
- ☀️ Exposure compensation (brighter/darker)
- 🔲 3×3 composition grid
- 🖼 The finished photo lands on the remote automatically (full quality stays in the camera's gallery)
- 🔋 Camera battery level shown on the remote
- 📞 An incoming call doesn't interrupt the session (runs in the background)
- 🔁 If the link drops, it reconnects to the remembered pair automatically

**Privacy first:** no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no internet requests. The app
collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Works offline anywhere — outdoors, on the road, on a shoot: wherever you have two phones.
